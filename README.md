# Hi there, I am Chris 👋

## About

ML scientist specializing in protein language models, structure-based design, and biological sequence analysis. Previously at LanzaTech, where I built protein ML pipelines leveraging ESM embeddings, AlphaFold-2, DiffDock, and MD simulation to accelerate enzyme engineering on HPC infrastructure.

Current projects focus on adapting pretrained models for non-model organisms: quantifying ESM-2 species bias, fine-tuning with LoRA, and training embedding decoders. See Projects below.

In university, I studied biophysics with a background in human genomics from Heather Wheeler's lab at Loyola University Chicago.

## Projects and Repositories

### Python Packages
- [Homolog Search Tools](https://github.com/chrisnguyen11/homolog-search-tools) - tools for protein sequence analysis: search protein databases for homologs, visualize search results, and perform sequence-based analysis for downstream applications.


### Data Science and ML Projects
- [Protein Language Modeling](https://github.com/chrisnguyen11/protein-language-modeling) — 
  A series of notebooks exploring applications of ESM-2 protein language models 
  for non-model organisms.
  1. Species Bias: Examines whether ESM-2 log-likelihoods differ between model and non-model organisms, finding that proteome composition rather than species-specific bias drives the difference.
  2. LoRA Fine-tuning: Fine-tunes ESM-2 on the *A. baylyi* proteome using LoRA, significantly improving log-likelihoods across all datasets.
  3. ESM-2 Decoder: Trains decoder models to reconstruct amino acid sequences from ESM-2 embeddings.
